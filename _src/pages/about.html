About

Dr. Mehbub Alam

Assistant Professor · Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Indian Institute of Information Technology Raichur, Karnataka, India · Since September 2025

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Raichur. Before joining, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Centre for Cyber-Physical Systems (C2PS), Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi, working on secure unmanned traffic management.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Information Technology Guwahati, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Rakesh Matam and Prof. Ferdous Ahmed Barbhuiya at the Design Innovation Centre (DIC) Lab. My doctoral work produced efficient task-offloading frameworks for fog-computing environments; my postdoctoral work moved into lightweight, context-aware authentication for drone Remote ID.
